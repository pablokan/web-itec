# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Prospective students, current students, and families in Río Cuarto and region seeking technical higher education (technicians in AI, software, mechatronics, tourism) or technical secondary education (Secundario Tecnológico Ada Byron) and continuing education (PCC).

## Product Purpose
To serve as the definitive digital gateway for Instituto Tecnológico Río Cuarto (iTec), showcasing 30+ years of educational trajectory, modern technical career offerings, virtual campus access, institutional identity, and admissions workflows.

## Positioning
An established regional institution of higher technical education combining decades of trajectory with cutting-edge technological programs (such as Artificial Intelligence) and a comprehensive technical secondary school.

## Operating Context
Users access the platform across desktop and mobile devices while exploring educational offerings, checking academic calendars and schedules, reviewing admission requirements, and submitting inquiry or enrollment forms.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Complete content inventory across 18 pages (carreras, estudiantes, ada-byron, trayectos, portal-de-trabajo, itec-labs).
- External integrations: Google Forms for admissions/inquiries, WhatsApp direct links, YouTube embeds, and Google Drive videos.
- Development constraint: Pure static HTML/CSS/JS with modular partials or standalone pages; current work involves iterating through experimental drafts (e.g., building a refined third draft landing page from scratch in a dedicated folder).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: iTec Río Cuarto (Instituto Tecnológico Río Cuarto)
- Secondary Institution: Secundario Tecnológico Ada Byron
- Tagline: Educación para transformar
- Assets: Verified institutional logos, 40th anniversary branding assets, faculty photographs, and course/career promotional banners.

## Evidence on Hand
- Full content inventory in `content/` and `content/_assets-manifest.md`.
- Existing assets in `site/assets/` (images, videos, fonts, icons).
- Previous iterations in `site/` and `site-minerva/`.

## Product Principles
1. **Clarity of Purpose**: Educational information must be immediately scannable and accessible to students and parents.
2. **Fidelity to Content**: All official program data, schedules, and contact channels must be preserved verbatim.
3. **Impeccable Craft**: Visual presentation must reflect modern technical excellence and institutional prestige.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility practices for responsive mobile and desktop viewing.
