# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective students (adolescents aged 12–15) and their families in Río Cuarto and region seeking a technical secondary education with a comprehensive, technology-oriented approach. Current students and their families also use the site for institutional information and contact.

## Product Purpose

To serve as the independent digital gateway for Secundario Tecnológico Ada Byron — a secondary school within iTec Río Cuarto — showcasing its integrative educational proposal (Programming and Robotics orientation alongside arts, literature, and human sciences), admission process, school life, and institutional contact.

## Positioning

A secondary school that combines technical rigor (programming, robotics, AI) with a humanistic, comprehensive education — not a purely technical track, but a formative experience that prepares students for both work and life. Namesake Ada Byron (countess of Lovelace, pioneer of computing) embodies this duality: mathematical precision + creative vision.

## Operating Context

Families research educational options for their children during the admission cycle (pre-inscripción via Google Forms, interviews, document submission). The school runs 1° and 2° year currently, opening 3° year in 2026. Physical locations: Sede Centro (Constitución 716) and Sede Campus (Tejerina Norte 783). Contact via WhatsApp and Instagram @adabyron.itec.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single landing page with all key information (propuesta, admisión, galería, contacto)
- Google Forms integration for pre-inscription
- WhatsApp contact links
- Instagram media (real student photos) as gallery content
- Pure static HTML/CSS/JS
- Must function independently from the main iTec site while maintaining a visual family resemblance

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Secundario Tecnológico Ada Byron / Colegio Ada Byron
- Parent institution: iTec Río Cuarto (Instituto Tecnológico Río Cuarto)
- Namesake: Ada Byron (Ada Lovelace) — pioneer of computing
- Primary accent: red #D72F32 (iTec brand color, family resemblance)
- Secondary accent: sky blue
- Logo: custom Ada Byron logo (logo-ada-byron.png)
- Orientation: Programming and Robotics with comprehensive education (arts, literature, human sciences)
- Tagline (implied): Tecnología con mirada humana
- Assets in /ada/img/: logo-ada-byron.png + 10 gallery photos of school life

## Evidence on Hand

- Instagram account @adabyron.itec (~52 posts with real student life photography)
- Current institutional page at itecriocuarto.org.ar/adabyron with full content (propuesta, admisión, quién fue Ada Byron, galería)
- Press article from Puntal (2023) announcing the school's opening
- Facebook page InstitutoAdaByron with student testimonial videos

## Product Principles

1. **Authenticity over stock**: Real photos of students, campus, and activities must lead the visual narrative — never generic educational imagery.
2. **Clarity for families**: Admission process, requirements, and contact information must be immediately scannable and actionable.
3. **Warm technology**: The school is technical but not cold — the visual system must feel approachable, youthful, and human.
4. **Independent identity**: While part of iTec, Ada Byron must stand as its own brand with its own voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility practices for responsive mobile and desktop viewing. Content must be legible for parents (potentially viewing on mobile) and engaging for prospective students.
